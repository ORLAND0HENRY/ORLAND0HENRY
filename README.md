## `01` — About Me

I'm Orlando Henry, a developer, technology enthusiast, and founder of **ORLANTECH INNOVATIONS**.

My focus is building useful digital solutions that address real problems. I work primarily with Python and Django, developing web applications and strengthening my understanding of the engineering principles behind them.

I enjoy the entire process: understanding a problem, designing a solution, writing the code, debugging what breaks, and refining the result until it works better.

I'm particularly interested in backend engineering, cybersecurity, automation, and the broader possibilities of artificial intelligence.

**My approach:** Understand the fundamentals. Build real things. Learn from the process & Keep improving.
